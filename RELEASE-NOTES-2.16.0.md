# NotchHarbor 2.16.0

- Screen-bounded menu-bar controls with a scrollable body and bottom Keys, Music and Mirror tabs.
- Spotify Desktop controls: current track, cover art, playback, seeking, volume, shuffle and repeat where supported. Connect explicitly; requires Automation approval. No Spotify credentials stored.
- Circular camera mirror, started manually, stopped when hidden or sleeping. Video preview only, no recording or microphone capture; requires Camera approval.
- Separate sidebar settings for General, Appearance, Notch Behavior, Keyboard Sounds, Music & Mirror and Updates.
- Adjustable hover/close delays, click-only opening, expanded width, accent and animation preference with Reduce Motion support.
- Existing preloaded keyboard audio, stale-event protection and 30-second idle suspension preserved.

Community build: ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. Input Monitoring, Camera or Automation may need approval after replacement. Spotify Desktop is required for music controls. Downloads/installation remain manual. No measured battery-life claim is made.

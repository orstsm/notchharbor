# NotchHarbor 2.16.1

- Playing Spotify tracks appear in the collapsed island: small cover on the left, a subtle playback indicator on the right, with the physical notch kept clear.
- Hover or click the now-playing island to open Music directly. Pausing or quitting Spotify restores the plain collapsed notch. Existing dwell/drag protection remains.
- Compact music layout: cover, title, artist, progress/time and previous/play-pause/next. Volume expands on demand; shuffle, repeat, refresh and Open Spotify are in the More menu.
- Successful Spotify connection is remembered. Island mode uses playback notifications even with the Music tab closed; no recurring Spotify polling. Sleep suspends observation; wake refreshes once. Menu-bar-only mode observes only while Music is visible.
- Playback bars are a low-rate visual indicator, not an audio visualizer or microphone analysis. Reduce Motion keeps them static.

Community build, ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple. macOS may require renewed Input Monitoring or Automation approval after replacement. No battery-life percentage is claimed.
